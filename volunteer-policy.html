Volunteer Policies

Texts, photos, video and code of conduct for MAA's Inspiration volunteers.

Texts, Photos & Video

  • By signing up and agreeing to our Volunteer Terms, Policies & Waiver, you agree to receive informational texts from Maa's Inspiration Inc. about volunteering, deliveries and programs. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out and HELP for help. Consent is not a condition of volunteering.
  • We take photos and videos at meal service, Maa’s Place and events and may use them on our website, social media and materials, without payment. We never sell images, use first names only, and never post phone numbers, addresses or other private details.
  • If you do not want to be photographed or featured, tell us at (469) 270-8156 or aslam@maasinspiration.org and we will honor it, and remove posts we control where possible.
  • Volunteers must not post photos of seniors, guests or meal recipients on personal accounts.

Volunteer Code of Conduct

  • Dignity first: treat every guest, senior, family and fellow volunteer with kindness and respect – Love All, Serve All.
  • Show up: arrive on time, check in and check out with the shift lead, and let us know early if you can't make it.
  • Food safety: wash hands, wear gloves and hair covering in the kitchen, and follow the lead's instructions.
  • Privacy: keep guests' personal information private. Do not take personal photos of guests; take photos only when Maa’s asks, and never of anyone who has opted out.
  • Safety: no alcohol, drugs, smoking or weapons at any site; drivers must have a valid license and their own auto insurance, which is their only coverage (Maa’s insurance does not cover drivers).
  • No harassment or discrimination of any kind. Minors volunteer only with guardian approval and adult supervision.
  • Ask for help: report any concern or injury to the shift lead or Aslam right away.

Questions? aslam@maasinspiration.org

LOVE ALL SERVE ALL

Serving our community with warmth, dignity, and compassion through nourishing meals and genuine care.
