Maa's Place Senior Center

A warm, welcoming place where seniors in Dallas–Fort Worth gather for activities, homestyle snacks, chai and companionship, carried forward in the spirit of Maa, whose greatest joy was feeding others.

Board gamesBingoKaraokeCraftsBeads & jewelry
