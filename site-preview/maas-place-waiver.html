Maa’s Place Participation Waiver and Release

The Maa’s Place is a free center for seniors operated by MAA's Inspiration Inc., a 501(c)(3) nonprofit located at 2700 Old Denton Rd, Suite 102, Carrollton, TX 75007.

1. Voluntary participation.

I am choosing to take part in Maa’s Place activities, which may include light exercise, chair yoga, walks, games, arts and crafts, outings, and meals or snacks. I understand that participation is voluntary and that I may stop at any time.

2. Health and fitness.

I confirm that I am able to take part in these activities. I will consult my doctor if I have any doubts, and I will tell staff about any health, mobility, or dietary needs. I understand that the Maa’s Place does not provide medical care, supervision for memory care, or transportation.

3. Risks.

I understand that physical activities and outings carry some risk of injury, including falls, strains, and illness. I accept these risks.

4. RELEASE OF LIABILITY.

I RELEASE MAA'S INSPIRATION INC., ITS DIRECTORS, VOLUNTEERS, AND STAFF FROM ANY CLAIMS FOR INJURY, ILLNESS, OR LOSS ARISING FROM MY PARTICIPATION, INCLUDING CLAIMS ARISING FROM THEIR OWN NEGLIGENCE, TO THE FULLEST EXTENT ALLOWED BY TEXAS LAW.

5. Emergencies.

In an emergency, I authorize staff to call 911 and contact my emergency contact. I am responsible for any resulting medical costs.

6. Food.

I will tell staff about any food allergies. Food may be prepared in a kitchen that also handles common allergens.

7. Texts, photos and video.

By signing, I agree to receive informational texts from Maa's Inspiration Inc. about its programs (message frequency varies, message and data rates may apply, reply STOP to opt out and HELP for help; consent is not a condition of services), and that Maa's may take photos and video at programs and events and use them on its website, social media and materials. If I do not want to be photographed or featured, I will tell Maa's at (469) 270-8156 or aslam@maasinspiration.org, and Maa's will honor it.

8. Signature.

I have read and understand this waiver.
