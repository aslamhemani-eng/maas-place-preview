Meal Recipient Terms & Policies

For seniors who get Saturday meal deliveries. Version meals-terms-v3.1-2026-10-19.

Questions? Call (469) 270-8156.

1. The service and delivery

  • Volunteer drivers bring a free meal to your door on Saturdays between 10 AM and noon.
  • The driver hands you the meal or leaves it as your notes say, and does not come inside unless there is an emergency.
  • If no one answers, the driver calls our coordinator and we may call you. Food is not left where it can spoil. If someone seems hurt or in danger, the driver calls 911.

2. Door photo and privacy

  • The driver takes one photo at every stop, showing only the meal at the door and the house number – never you or the inside of your home.
  • Photos are kept for 1 week, then deleted, and are never posted or shared.
  • We use your information only to deliver your meals. We do not sell or share it. See our Privacy Policy.

3. Food and changes

  • Food may be made in a kitchen that also handles common allergens, and is not made for special medical diets. Please call us about any serious food allergy.
  • Call (469) 270-8156 if your address or phone changes, or to pause or stop deliveries.
  • By agreeing, you confirm your information is true. Someone helping you may agree for you, with your permission.
