Meal Recipient Terms & Policies

For seniors who get Saturday meal deliveries. Version meals-terms-v3-2026-10-19.

Questions? Call (469) 270-8156.

1. About Maa’s Meals

  • Volunteer drivers bring a meal to your door on Saturdays between 10 AM and noon.
  • There is no charge.
  • Meals are delivered when volunteers and food are available. We will call you if a delivery must change.

2. Delivery

  • The driver hands you the meal or leaves it where your delivery notes say.
  • Drivers do not come inside your home unless there is an emergency.
  • If no one answers, the driver follows our steps and calls our coordinator, and we may call you. The driver will not leave food where it can spoil unless your notes say so.
  • If a driver sees that someone looks hurt, very sick or in danger, they will call 911.

3. Door photo at every stop

  • The driver takes one delivery photo at every stop. It shows the meal at the door and the house number only – never you, the inside of your home, or a license plate.
  • Photos are taken only in the Maa’s app, are never posted or shared, and are kept for 1 week, then deleted.

4. Food

  • Food may be prepared in a kitchen that also handles common allergens, such as nuts, milk, eggs and wheat.
  • Meals are not made for special medical diets. Please call us about any serious food allergy.

5. Privacy

  • We use your name, address, phone number and delivery notes only to deliver your meals and contact you about them.
  • Only authorized Maa’s staff and volunteers see your information, and drivers keep it private.
  • We do not sell or share your information. See our Privacy Policy.

6. Changes

  • Please tell us if your address, phone or delivery needs change.
  • To pause or stop deliveries, call (469) 270-8156.

7. Your agreement

By agreeing, you confirm the information on your form is true as far as you know, and you agree to these terms. If someone helped you fill out the form, they agree on your behalf with your permission.
