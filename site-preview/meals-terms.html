Meal Recipient Terms, Policies & Waiver

For seniors who get Saturday meal deliveries. Version meals-terms-v3.4-2026-10-19.

Questions? Call (469) 270-8156.

Maa’s Meals is a free meal delivery program of Maa’s Inspiration Inc., a 501(c)(3) nonprofit. By agreeing to these terms you also agree to the consents below. You can opt out of texts and photos at any time (see section 9).

1. The service and delivery

  • Volunteer drivers bring a free meal to your door on Saturdays between 10 AM and noon.
  • The driver hands you the meal or leaves it as your notes say, and does not come inside unless there is an emergency.
  • If no one answers, the driver calls our coordinator and we may call you. Food is not left where it can spoil.

2. Text messages

  • By giving your phone number and agreeing to these terms, you agree to receive informational texts from Maa’s Inspiration Inc. about your deliveries and our programs, such as delivery reminders.
  • Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help. Texts are not a condition of getting meals. See our Text Message Terms.

3. Door photo (kept 7 days)

  • The driver takes one photo at every stop to confirm delivery. It shows only the meal at the door and the house number – never you or the inside of your home.
  • Door photos are kept for 7 days, then deleted. They are never posted or shared.

4. Your address and information

  • We share your name, address, phone and delivery notes only with the volunteer driver assigned to your delivery, and only for that delivery. Drivers agree to keep it private.
  • We do not sell or share your information with anyone else for marketing. See our Privacy Policy.

5. Food safety and allergies

  • Meals are made with care, but may be made in a kitchen that also handles common allergens (such as nuts, milk, eggs, wheat and soy). They are not made for special medical diets.
  • Please tell us about any serious food allergy. Eat or refrigerate your meal soon after delivery, and do not eat food that looks or smells spoiled.

6. Emergencies

If a driver finds that someone seems hurt, very sick or in danger, you agree that the driver may call 911 and tell our coordinator. If we have a family or emergency contact for you, we may let them know.

7. Photos and video at events

  • If you come to a Maa’s event, we may take photos and video and use them on our website, social media and materials. Tell us if you do not want to be photographed and we will honor it.

8. Release

TO THE EXTENT TEXAS LAW ALLOWS, YOU RELEASE MAA’S INSPIRATION INC. AND ITS DIRECTORS, STAFF, VOLUNTEERS AND DRIVERS FROM CLAIMS FOR ILLNESS, ALLERGIC REACTION, INJURY OR LOSS ARISING FROM THE MEALS OR THEIR DELIVERY.

9. How to opt out

  • Texts: reply STOP to any text, or call us. Reply HELP for help.
  • Photos, video and anything else in these terms you can opt out of: call (469) 270-8156 or email aslam@maasinspiration.org. Tell us what you want to stop and we will honor it.
  • Opting out does not affect your services or your place in the program.

10. Changes and your agreement

  • Call (469) 270-8156 if your address or phone changes, or to pause or stop deliveries.
  • By agreeing, you confirm your information is true. Someone helping you may agree for you, with your permission.
