Driver Terms, Policies & Waiver

For volunteers who deliver Maa’s Meals in their own vehicles. Version driver-terms-v3.3-2026-10-19.

Questions? Call (469) 270-8156.

1. Who can drive

  • You must be at least 18 years old.
  • You must hold a valid Texas driver’s license.
  • You must carry current auto liability insurance on the vehicle you use that meets at least the Texas minimum: $30,000 for injury to one person, $60,000 for injury to two or more people, and $25,000 for property damage.
  • Keep the vehicle registered and in safe working condition (lights, brakes, tires, wipers, seat belts).
  • Keep your license and insurance current. Tell Maa’s right away if either one lapses, is suspended, revoked or cancelled, or expires.
  • You do not need to send us copies. Maa’s may ask to see your license and insurance if needed.

2. Insurance and liability

  • Your own auto insurance is primary and is the only coverage while you drive for Maa’s. If there is an accident while you are volunteering, your insurance is expected to pay.
  • Maa’s Inspiration Inc. does not provide auto, liability or accident insurance for volunteer drivers.
  • You confirm that your auto insurance meets at least the Texas minimums: $30,000 / $60,000 / $25,000.
  • Texas law that protects volunteers from some lawsuits does not fully protect volunteers who are driving, because auto insurance is expected to cover driving. Check with your insurance company that delivering meals as an unpaid volunteer is covered.

3. Safe driving and phone use

  • Obey all traffic laws and wear your seat belt.
  • Do not text or read messages while driving. Texas law bans it. Look at the app, call or take photos only when parked.
  • Do not drive after drinking alcohol or using drugs or medicine that make you drowsy.
  • Do not drive when you are too tired, sick, or the weather is unsafe. Tell the coordinator if you cannot drive.
  • Do not give rides to meal recipients or other people as part of Maa’s Meals unless Maa’s has approved it in writing.
  • Keep the meals in the insulated bags Maa’s gives you and deliver them on time.

4. At the door

  • Be friendly and respectful, and wear your Maa’s name badge (if provided).
  • Do not go inside a recipient’s home unless there is an emergency.
  • Photo at every stop: take one delivery photo at every stop. It shows the meal at the door and the house number only – never the person, the inside of the home, or a license plate. Use only the Maa’s app, not your phone’s camera roll, and never post or share delivery photos. Maa’s keeps delivery photos for 1 week, then deletes them.
  • If no one answers, follow the app steps and call the coordinator. Do not leave food where it can spoil unless the recipient’s instructions say so.

5. Privacy

Recipients’ names, addresses, phone numbers and delivery photos are private. Use them only to deliver meals. Do not share, copy, post or keep them after your route, and do not contact recipients for personal reasons.

6. Emergencies and reporting

  • If someone looks hurt, very sick or in danger, call 911, then the coordinator.
  • If you think an older adult is being abused, neglected or exploited, you must report it to the Texas Abuse Hotline: 1-800-252-5400, and tell the coordinator.

7. Accidents, tickets and problems

  1. Stop safely. Call 911 if anyone is hurt. Follow the law for exchanging information.
  2. Call the Maa’s Meals coordinator as soon as it is safe.
  3. Report the accident to your own insurance company.
  4. Tell Maa’s in writing within 24 hours about any accident, injury or traffic ticket that happened while volunteering, and within 7 days if you get a ticket or your license changes at any other time.

8. Costs

Volunteer driving is unpaid. Maa’s Inspiration Inc. does not pay for gas, mileage, tolls, parking, tickets or vehicle damage unless approved in writing before the trip. Some mileage for charity driving may be tax-deductible – ask your tax adviser.

9. Driver Waiver and Release of Liability

Please read carefully. This section affects your legal rights.

  • Assumption of risk. I understand that driving and delivering meals carry risks, including traffic accidents, injury, illness and damage to my vehicle or property. I choose to volunteer and accept these risks.
  • Release and hold harmless. To the fullest extent allowed by Texas law, I release and hold harmless Maa’s Inspiration Inc. and its volunteers, board members and partners from any claims for injury, illness, loss or damage arising from my volunteer driving.
  • My vehicle and my driving. I am responsible for my own vehicle, its condition and its insurance, and for any traffic tickets, fines or accidents while I drive.
  • No compensation. I am an unpaid volunteer. I will not receive pay, mileage or other compensation unless approved in writing before the trip.
  • Emergency medical care. If I am hurt or ill while volunteering, I allow Maa’s to call 911 and get emergency medical care for me. I am responsible for the cost of that care.
  • Read and understood. I have read and understood this waiver and release, and I sign it voluntarily.

10. Your agreement

By agreeing, you confirm that you are 18 or older, that you hold a valid Texas driver’s license and current auto insurance that meets the Texas minimums, that you will keep them current, and that you will tell Maa’s if either one lapses. You agree to follow these rules and to the Driver Waiver and Release of Liability. Maa’s Inspiration Inc. may stop your driving role at any time for safety reasons.

11. Texts, Photos & Video

  • Texts: by giving a phone number and agreeing to these terms, you agree to receive informational text messages from Maa’s Inspiration Inc. about volunteering, deliveries and programs. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out and HELP for help. Consent is not a condition of receiving services. See our Text Message Terms.
  • Photos and video: Maa’s may take photos and video at programs and events and use them on its website, social media and materials. If you do not want to be photographed or featured, tell us at (469) 270-8156 or aslam@maasinspiration.org and we will honor it.
  • Delivery door photos are for delivery confirmation only. They are deleted after 7 days and never published.
