Volunteer Code of Conduct

Code of conduct, sign-up checklist and event checklists for every volunteer. Draft v2 · October 19, 2026.

⬇ Download PDF

Questions? Call (469) 270-8156.

This is the code of conduct in our Volunteer Terms, Policies & Waiver (volunteer-terms-v3.6-2026-10-19). When you agree to those terms in the app or on the website, you agree to this code. Post a copy at every site.

1. Dignity first

  • Treat every guest, senior, family and fellow volunteer with kindness and respect.
  • No harassment or discrimination of any kind.

2. Show up

  • Arrive on time. Check in and check out with the shift lead or the Maa's app.
  • Can't come? Tell us as early as you can: reply to the text or call (469) 270-8156.

3. Food safety

  • Wash your hands. Wear gloves and a hair covering when handling food.
  • Snacks and chai volunteers must have a food handler card.
  • Follow the lead's instructions. Stay home if you are sick.

4. Privacy, photos and video

  • Keep guests' names, addresses, phone numbers and health notes private.
  • No personal photos of guests. Take photos only when Maa's asks, and never of anyone on the photo opt-out list.
  • Do not post photos of seniors, guests or meal recipients on your own accounts.

5. Safety

  • No alcohol, drugs, smoking or weapons at any site.
  • Drivers also follow the Driver Terms (18+, valid license, their own auto insurance, which is their only coverage).
  • Maa's Place does not provide rides to the center or to outings. Do not offer rides as a Maa's volunteer.
  • Maa's Place and Bloom Village volunteers must be 18 or older. At Maa's Meals, volunteers under 18 serve only with a parent or guardian's approval and adult supervision. The shift lead must be 18 or older.

6. Ask for help

  • Report any concern, injury or accident to the shift lead or Aslam right away. In an emergency, call 911 first.
  • Suspect abuse, neglect or exploitation? Tell the shift lead and call the Texas Abuse Hotline, 1‑800‑252‑5400.

Not following this code may mean we ask you to stop volunteering.

No separate consent forms

Texts, photos and video are part of the Volunteer Terms. Agreeing to the terms counts as consent unless the person opts out: reply STOP to a text, or call (469) 270-8156. Texts are not a condition of volunteering.

No paper sign-ups. Every volunteer signs up in the Maa's app or on the website. No phone? A staff member helps them sign up on a Maa's phone or tablet.

Volunteer Event Checklists

For Saturday Maa's Meals, the first volunteer orientation (Saturday, Oct 24), and other volunteer events. Saturday volunteer check-in is at 9:30 AM. Tick each box. The shift lead (18 or older) keeps this sheet.

Event: Date: Shift lead:

Location: Start / end time:

Before (the week before and the morning of)

  • Thursday: availability text sent; replies counted (1 = deliver, 2 = packing). Maa's Meals: drivers assigned, routes about 12 stops each, published.
  • Recipient list updated (new, paused, moved). Packing list and package count set.
  • Supplies checked: food, containers, bags, labels, gloves, hair coverings, hand soap, sanitizer.
  • First-aid kit, blank Incident Reports, emergency numbers and exits checked.
  • Printed: code of conduct (posted), name tags, pens.
  • Photo opt-out list printed or open in the app, for whoever takes photos.
  • Check-in ready by 9:30 AM on Saturdays: Maa's app check-in (phone).
  • Cash box, receipt book and count sheet ready only if money may be collected (see the Cash & Check Handling Procedure).

During

  • Every volunteer checks in (in the app; 9:30 AM on Saturdays). New volunteers have agreed to the Volunteer Terms in the app or on the website before they start.
  • Short huddle: tasks, food safety, privacy and photos, where the first-aid kit and exits are, who to tell about problems.
  • Food safety: handwashing, gloves, hair coverings; snacks and chai volunteers have a food handler card; sick volunteers sent home kindly.
  • Drivers out with routes. Door photos only through the Maa's app. No-answer stops reported.
  • Photos only by the assigned person, never of anyone who has opted out.
  • Any injury, accident or concern: 911 first if needed, then the shift lead fills an Incident Report the same day.

After

  • No-answer stops followed up (call, redeliver or carry over). Delivery photos and problem reports reviewed.
  • Every volunteer checked out; hours recorded.
  • Totals recorded: packages delivered, volunteers, hours. Supplies put away and restock needs noted.
  • Any cash or checks: two-person count, logged, locked up, deposited within 2 business days.
  • Volunteers thanked. Incident Reports and follow-ups handed to the program lead.
