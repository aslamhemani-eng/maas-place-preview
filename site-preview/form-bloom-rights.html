Bloom Village Participant Rights

Please read your rights, one page at a time. A staff member or family member can read them aloud with you. At the end, sign to say you received them.

Need help? Call (469) 270-8156.

Your rights as a person

You are an adult. You have the same rights as everyone else. Bloom Village must respect them. These rights come from Texas law (Human Resources Code §102.003) and HHSC rules (26 TAC §559.228).

  • You have all the rights of any person under Texas and U.S. law. No one may stop you, pressure you, treat you unfairly or punish you for using your rights.
  • You will be treated with dignity and respect, no matter your race, religion, national origin, sex, age, disability, marital status or how your services are paid for.
  • You may make your own choices about your life, care, benefits and services.
  • If you need help with decisions, you may choose a guardian or representative to help you.

Safety

  • You have the right to be free from abuse, neglect and exploitation.
  • You have the right to be free from physical and mental abuse, including punishment and restraints (holding or medicine) used to discipline you or for staff convenience. Texas law allows restraints only if a doctor authorizes them in writing or in an emergency to keep you or others from injury. Bloom Village does not use restraints.
  • If you have a court-appointed guardian, you may take part in a behavior program that uses restraints or unpleasant stimuli only if your guardian gives informed consent. Bloom Village does not run such programs.
  • You have the right to services in a safe and clean place.

Talking, privacy and choices

Communication and complaints

  • You may talk in your own language with others and with staff.
  • You may complain about your care or treatment. You may complain without giving your name, or someone you choose can complain for you. We must respond quickly to fix the problem, and we may not punish you or treat you differently because you complained.

Privacy

  • You have the right to privacy when using the restroom or getting help with personal care.
  • You may have a private place to visit with people, if it does not take away other people’s rights.
  • Your phone calls and written messages are private. You may send and receive mail unopened.
  • Your records are private. You may see your own records. We share them only with your permission, with another provider if you transfer, or when the law requires it.

Choices, activities and visitors

  • You may take part in social, religious and community groups and activities.
  • You may control your own schedule and activities, have your own food at any time, have visitors you choose at any time, and get into the building. These can be limited only if your plan from your program provider says so, based on an assessed need.
  • You may say no to a scheduled activity. We will write down that you chose not to take part. You may refuse to do work for Bloom Village.
  • You may keep and use your own belongings, as space allows. Some items may be limited for everyone’s safety.
  • You may manage your own money, or choose someone in writing to help you. Choosing a helper does not take away your other rights.

Your health, money and leaving

Your health and your plan

  • We will tell you, in words you understand, about your health as it relates to our services, and tell you when we notice a big change.
  • You may choose your own doctor, and be told ahead of time about care that may affect you.
  • You may take part in your plan (IPC, IPP, PDP or implementation plan) and get services that follow it, from staff who respond to your needs.
  • You may refuse medical treatment after being told what could happen and saying that you understand.
  • If you dislike or disagree with our services, we will help you raise it with your program provider.
  • You may make a living will, a medical power of attorney, or choose a guardian ahead of time.

Money and benefits

  • Within 30 days after you start, we will tell you whether you may be entitled to Medicare or Medicaid benefits and which of our items and services those benefits cover, including anything we may not charge you for.

Leaving the program

  • We may end your services only if: it is for your welfare and we cannot meet your needs; you no longer need services; your safety or someone else’s would be in danger; we close or stop taking part in the program that pays for your care; or you do not pay after reasonable notices.
  • Before ending services because we cannot meet your needs, we will try to meet your needs, talk with you, your legal representative and your program provider, and tell you in writing before you are discharged. Except in an emergency, we give written notice at least 30 days ahead with the reason, the date, and how to appeal.

Your responsibilities

  • Treat other participants, staff and visitors with respect.
  • Tell us about health, allergy or support changes so we can keep you safe.
  • Follow safety rules, such as fire drills.
  • Your family or representative: sign in and out, keep phone numbers current, and pay fees as agreed.

How to make a complaint

A complaint (grievance) is a concern about anything at Bloom Village. You will never be punished for complaining. You can get help from anyone you choose, and you can complain without giving your name.

Step 1 – Tell someone

Tell any staff member, the administrator (Yasmeen Rajwani) or the director in person, by phone, in writing, by text or email, or in the way you communicate best. A family member, friend, legal representative or advocate can do it for you. You can also use our online complaint form.

Step 2 – We write it down and answer

The administrator tells you we received it within 2 business days and gives you a written answer within 10 business days. If it is about safety, we act right away.

Step 3 – If you are not happy with the answer

Ask for a review by the director of Maa’s Inspiration Inc., Aslam Hemani, at (469) 270-8156. The director answers in writing within 10 business days.

You can go outside Bloom Village at any time

  • HHSC Complaint and Incident Intake: 1-800-458-9858
  • Texas Abuse Hotline: 1-800-252-5400
  • Disability Rights Texas: 1-800-252-9108
  • Emergency: 911
