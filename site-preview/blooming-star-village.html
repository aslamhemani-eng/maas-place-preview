Our Mission

Every person deserves a place where they are understood, included and helped to grow. A free day program for recreation and care, Blooming Star Village carries Maa's spirit of unconditional care to neurodiverse individuals and the families who love them, offering a calm, supportive community so no family has to find its way alone.

🌱 Belonging

A place where differences are welcomed, not just accommodated.

🤝 Family support

Caring for the whole family, including parents, siblings and caregivers.

⭐ Growth

Helping each person build skills and confidence at their own pace.

Who We Serve

Blooming Star Village is planned for individuals with sensory processing differences, autism, developmental delays and other special needs, together with their families, across Dallas–Fort Worth.

Ages served: to confirm · Eligibility / referral needed?: to confirm

What Your Child Will Do

Each day blends play, routine and gentle skill-building, at each child's own pace.

🧩 Sensory Play & Movement

Swinging, climbing, balance and hands-on sensory activities that help the body and mind feel calm and ready.

💬 Friendship & Communication

Small, friendly groups to practice taking turns, sharing, expressing feelings and making friends.

🎨 Arts, Music & Stories

Painting, crafts, music, movement and story time to spark creativity and joy.

🗓️ Daily Routine & Life Skills

A predictable, visual daily routine with simple everyday skills that build independence.

🌳 Active & Outdoor Play

Games, ball play and outdoor time to build strength, coordination and confidence.

👨‍👩‍👧 Family & Caregiver Support

A few hours of peace of mind for parents, plus a friendly community of families who understand.

🍎 Snacks

Healthy snacks in the MAA's Inspiration tradition, with care for dietary and sensory needs.

What a Visit Looks Like

1. Reach out

3. A plan that fits

4. Join in

Start attending at a pace that feels right.

FAQ

What are the hours?

Monday to Thursday, 9 AM to 2 PM.

When will Blooming Star Village open?

Opening date

Where is it located?

2700 Old Denton Rd, Suite 102, Carrollton, TX 75006 (same location as MAA's Inspiration)

What ages do you serve?

Ages served

Is there a cost?

No. Blooming Star Village is free.

Is this therapy?

No. Blooming Star Village is a day program for recreation and care: fun, inclusive activities and community. It does not provide therapy or medical services.

Do you provide transportation?

No, families arrange their own transportation.
