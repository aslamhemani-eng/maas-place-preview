Maa’s Place Volunteer Roles

The six volunteer roles at Maa’s Place and what each one does. Draft v2 · October 19, 2026.

Questions? Call (469) 270-8156.

All roles are volunteer (no pay; Volunteer Terms). Every volunteer agrees to the Volunteer Terms, Policies & Waiver and the code of conduct and attends orientation before their first shift. Shift times are a proposal for Aslam to confirm.

Must-know for every role

  • No outing transport. Maa's Place does not provide rides to the center or to outings. Seniors arrange their own way there and back. Outings are optional and covered by the senior's enrollment waiver. Do not offer rides as a Maa's volunteer.
  • Emergency plan. Call 911 first, then tell the shift lead. Know the exits, the meeting point and where the first-aid kit is (page 3). Fill in an Incident Report the same day.
  • Photo opt-out list. Before any photo, the shift lead checks the list. Never photograph anyone on it. First names only. No photos on personal accounts.
  • Privacy. Seniors' names, phones, addresses and health notes stay in the private enrollment records only. Don't leave lists out.
  • Not a care program. No medical or personal care and no medicines. Help is kind and respectful; ask before helping.
  • Abuse, neglect or exploitation? Tell the shift lead and call the Texas Abuse Hotline, 1‑800‑252‑5400.
  • Closed Thursday, November 26 (Thanksgiving).

The roles

1. Shift lead (in charge for the day)

  • Opens and closes the center, and is the person in charge for safety, questions and problems.
  • Holds the daily huddle (9:45 AM): who is doing what, today's activities, any seniors who need extra help.
  • Checks the photo opt-out list before any photo, and keeps the private records private.
  • Handles emergencies and calls emergency contacts. Files Incident Reports and reports the day's attendance count.

Shift: 9:15 AM – 2:30 PM · Per day: 1 (must be 18 or older)

Must know: Emergency plan and exits; the kiosk staff PIN (never share it); who to call: Aslam or the backup.

2. Front desk / check-in kiosk helper

  • Welcomes each senior by name and helps with the front desk iPad: type your first name, tap your name, tap Yes.
  • If someone can't find their name, they tap “I'm new” or use the paper sign-in, and get the Maa's Place Enrollment, Terms & Waiver (or the website form).
  • Answers simple questions (hours, today's activity) and hands visitors to the shift lead.
  • The screen shows first name and last initial only and clears itself. Only the shift lead opens check-in with the PIN.

Shift: 9:45 AM – 2:00 PM, or two halves · Per day: 1 – 2

Must know: Seniors arrange their own rides; the photo opt-out list stays with the shift lead; never read out private details.

3. Activity lead

  • Leads the planned activity: chair exercise, walks, games, crafts, music, talks or tech help.
  • Explains each step clearly, offers easier options, and lets anyone sit out.
  • Sets up and puts away the activity supplies.
  • Tells the shift lead about anyone who seems unwell, dizzy or upset.

Shift: 10:00 AM – 2:00 PM (or one activity block) · Per day: 1 – 2

Must know: Stop the activity if someone feels unwell; no photos unless the shift lead says so; outings are optional and seniors get there on their own.

4. Snacks & chai

  • Prepares and serves snacks, tea and chai from the chai station.
  • Washes hands, wears gloves and a hair covering, and keeps hot drinks away from walkways.
  • Labels what is in each snack, offers low-sugar options, and asks about allergies.
  • Cleans the station and notes what needs restocking.
  • Must have a food handler card before their first snacks & chai shift.

Shift: 9:45 AM – 1:30 PM · Per day: 1 – 2

Must know: We cannot promise any food is allergen-free; hot liquids are a burn risk; a food handler card is required.

5. Setup & cleanup

  • Setup: chairs with arms, tables, clear walkways, activity and quiet areas, chai station.
  • Cleanup: wipe tables, put supplies away, empty trash, check restrooms, leave walkways clear.
  • Reports anything broken, slippery or unsafe to the shift lead right away.

Shift: Setup 9:15 – 10:15 AM · Cleanup 1:45 – 2:45 PM · Per day: 1 – 3

Must know: Keep exits and walkways clear at all times; lift safely and ask for help with heavy items.

6. Floater

  • Fills in wherever help is needed: front desk, activity, snacks or cleanup.
  • Keeps company with seniors who would like to chat, and helps people find a seat.
  • Covers breaks so other volunteers can rest.

Shift: 10:00 AM – 2:00 PM · Per day: 0 – 2

Must know: Same as every role; follow the shift lead's directions.

Before a volunteer's first shift

  • Agreed to the Volunteer Terms, Policies & Waiver (online or paper backup).
  • Attended volunteer orientation: senior safety, first aid basics, emergency plan, privacy.
  • Walked through the space: exits, meeting point, first-aid kit, restrooms.
  • Snacks & chai volunteers: food handler card on file. Shift leads: 18 or older.
