What We Do

Every activity at the Maa’s Place is chosen to keep seniors active in body and mind, from gentle exercise and brain games to arts, music, and learning something new. Just as important, it’s a place to share laughter, snacks, and friendship, where every senior feels valued and no one is alone.

💪 Physical

  • Chair exercises and gentle stretching
  • Balance and fall-prevention exercises
  • Group walks
  • Light dance and movement to music
  • Chair yoga or tai chi
  • Fun movement games (balloon toss, bean bag games)
  • Healthy snacks
  • Health checks and wellness talks

🧠 Mental

  • Puzzles, word games, and trivia
  • Memory and brain-training games
  • Reading and storytelling circles
  • Learning sessions (technology, health talks, new skills)
  • Music, singing, and sing-alongs
  • Arts and crafts for creativity and focus
  • Group discussions and sharing life stories
  • Bingo, cards, and board games

📚 Educational

  • Smartphone, tablet, and video-call help (staying in touch with family)
  • Health, nutrition, and medication-safety talks
  • Scam and fraud awareness
  • Guest speakers from the community
  • Cooking and healthy-eating demonstrations
  • Language and conversation classes
  • Gardening and nature sessions
  • Cultural and history discussions

🎲 Fun & Entertainment

  • Bingo, cards, and board games
  • Movie and music afternoons
  • Sing-alongs and karaoke
  • Birthday celebrations
  • Holiday and cultural festivals
  • Talent shows and storytelling
  • Outings and field trips
  • Tea, snacks, and social hours

🍵 Snacks & Chai

  • Light snacks
  • Tea and coffee
  • Fresh fruit
  • Special treats for birthdays and holidays
  • Prepared with care for senior dietary needs
