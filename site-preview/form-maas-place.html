Read the Maa’s Place Terms, Policies & Waiver

Maa’s Place is a free center for adults 55 and up, run by Maa’s Inspiration Inc., a 501(c)(3) nonprofit at 2700 Old Denton Rd, Suite 102, Carrollton, TX 75007. Open Monday to Thursday, 10 AM to 2 PM. By agreeing to these terms you also agree to the waiver and consents below. You can opt out of texts and photos at any time (see section 10).

1. Taking part

Activities may include light exercise, chair yoga, walks, games, arts and crafts, and snacks. Taking part is voluntary and you may stop at any time. I am able to take part, I will ask my doctor if I have doubts, and I will tell staff about any health, mobility or dietary needs. Maa’s Place does not provide medical care, memory care supervision or transportation.

2. Participation Waiver & Release

  • Assumption of risk. Physical activities, games and outings carry some risk of injury, including falls, strains and illness. I understand and accept these risks.
  • Release. I RELEASE MAA’S INSPIRATION INC., ITS DIRECTORS, VOLUNTEERS AND STAFF FROM ANY CLAIMS FOR INJURY, ILLNESS OR LOSS ARISING FROM MY PARTICIPATION, INCLUDING CLAIMS ARISING FROM THEIR OWN NEGLIGENCE, TO THE FULLEST EXTENT ALLOWED BY TEXAS LAW.

3. Emergency medical consent

In an emergency, I authorize Maa’s staff and volunteers to call 911 and to get emergency medical care for me. I am responsible for any medical costs.

4. Sharing information with my emergency contact

If I am hurt, become ill, or staff are worried about my safety, Maa’s may contact my emergency contact or family and share what they need to know to help me. Maa’s does not share my information with anyone else except when the law requires it.

5. Outings and transportation

Maa’s Place does not provide transportation. If Maa’s offers an outing, it is optional, Maa’s will tell me the details first, and this waiver and release also cover outings. I get myself to and from Maa’s Place and any outing unless Maa’s tells me otherwise in writing.

6. Food and snacks

Snacks, chai and meals may be prepared in a kitchen that also handles common allergens (such as nuts, milk, eggs, wheat and soy) and are not made for special medical diets. I will tell staff about any food allergy and choose what I eat.

7. Text messages

  • By giving my phone number and agreeing to these terms, I agree to receive informational texts from Maa’s Inspiration Inc. about Maa’s Place and our programs. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help. Texts are not a condition of taking part. See our Text Message Terms.

8. Photos and video

  • Maa’s may take photos and video at Maa’s Place and events and use them on its website, social media and materials, using first names only. If I do not want to be photographed or featured, I will tell Maa’s and they will honor it.

9. Signing for someone else

A family member or guardian may sign for the participant and agrees to these terms for them.

10. How to opt out

  • Texts: reply STOP to any text, or call us. Reply HELP for help.
  • Photos, video and anything else in these terms you can opt out of: call (469) 270-8156 or email aslam@maasinspiration.org. Tell us what you want to stop and we will honor it.
  • Opting out does not affect your services or your place in the program.
