Volunteer Terms & Policies

Code of conduct and photo policy for volunteers. Version volunteer-terms-v3.1-2026-10-19.

Questions? Call (469) 270-8156.

1. Code of conduct

  • Dignity first: treat every guest, senior, family and fellow volunteer with kindness and respect – Love All, Serve All.
  • Show up: arrive on time, check in and check out with the shift lead, and let us know early if you can’t make it.
  • Food safety: wash hands, wear gloves and hair covering in the kitchen, and follow the lead’s instructions.
  • Privacy: keep guests’ personal information private; no photos of guests without consent.
  • Safety: no alcohol, drugs, smoking or weapons at any site. Drivers must also agree to the Driver Terms & Policies.
  • No harassment or discrimination of any kind. Minors volunteer only with guardian approval and adult supervision.
  • Ask for help: report any concern or injury to the shift lead or Aslam right away.

2. Photos and videos

  • We take photos and videos at meal service, Maa’s Place and events to share our work on our website and social media.
  • Ticking the photo box means Maa’s Inspiration Inc. may use photos and videos that include you for these purposes, without payment. We never sell images and use your first name only.
  • Photo consent is optional. You can withdraw it any time by emailing aslam@maasinspiration.org.
  • Volunteers must not post photos of seniors, guests or meal recipients on personal accounts.

3. Bloom Village

Bloom Village begins only after its HHSC license is issued. Bloom Village volunteers must be 18 or older and complete screening and training. We will contact you.

4. Text messages

Text reminders are optional and need a separate Text Message Consent.
