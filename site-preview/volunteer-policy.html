Volunteer Policies

Photo consent and code of conduct for MAA's Inspiration volunteers.

Photo & Video Consent

  • We take photos and videos at meal service, the Maa’s Place and events to share our work on our website and social media (Facebook, Instagram, TikTok).
  • Ticking the photo consent box means MAA's Inspiration may use photos/videos that include you for these purposes, without payment. We never sell images.
  • With your permission we may use your first name only. We never post phone numbers, addresses or other private details.
  • Consent is optional – you can still volunteer without it. You can withdraw consent any time by emailing aslam@maasinspiration.org; we will stop using new images and remove posts we control where possible.
  • Seniors and participants are only filmed with their own written consent. Volunteers must not post photos of seniors, guests or meal recipients on personal accounts.

Volunteer Code of Conduct

  • Dignity first: treat every guest, senior, family and fellow volunteer with kindness and respect – Love All, Serve All.
  • Show up: arrive on time, check in and check out with the shift lead, and let us know early if you can't make it.
  • Food safety: wash hands, wear gloves and hair covering in the kitchen, and follow the lead's instructions.
  • Privacy: keep guests' personal information private; no photos of guests without consent.
  • Safety: no alcohol, drugs, smoking or weapons at any site; drivers must have a valid license and insurance.
  • No harassment or discrimination of any kind. Minors volunteer only with guardian approval and adult supervision.
  • Ask for help: report any concern or injury to the shift lead or Aslam right away.

Questions? aslam@maasinspiration.org

LOVE ALL SERVE ALL

Serving our community with warmth, dignity, and compassion through nourishing meals and genuine care.
