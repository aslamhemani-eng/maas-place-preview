Maa’s Place & Bloom Village Emergency Plan

What to do in a fire, medical emergency, severe weather, lockdown, lost participant or power outage. Draft v1 · October 19, 2026.

⬇ Download PDF

Questions? Call (469) 270-8156.

In any emergency

Call 911 first. Give the address: 2700 Old Denton Rd, Suite 102, Carrollton, TX 75007.

Then call Maa's office: (469) 270-8156.

This plan covers both programs in Suite 102. Maa's Place (senior center) and Bloom Village have separate areas; each program's person in charge leads their own area and they help each other. Every staff member and volunteer reads this plan at orientation.

Who is in charge

  • Maa's Place: the shift lead on duty.
  • Bloom Village: the administrator, Yasmeen Rajwani; backup Anila Hemani.
  • The person in charge brings the attendance list (kiosk or sign-in) to every evacuation and does the head count.

1. Fire or smoke

  1. Shout “Fire!” and pull the fire alarm if there is one.
  2. Move everyone out by the nearest safe exit. Help people who walk slowly; do not use the elevator.
  3. Close doors behind you. Do not go back for belongings.
  4. Go to the outside meeting spot and do a head count with the attendance list.
  5. Call 911 from outside. Then call (469) 270-8156.
  6. Use an extinguisher only on a small fire, if you are trained and have a clear way out.
  7. Nobody goes back inside until the fire department says it is safe.

2. Medical emergency

  1. Check the person. If they are not breathing, unconscious, have chest pain, signs of a stroke, a bad fall or heavy bleeding: call 911 now.
  2. Send someone for the first-aid kit and AED (if there is one).
  3. Stay with the person. Do not move them after a fall unless they are in danger.
  4. Send someone to the door to meet the ambulance.
  5. Call the person's emergency contact. Then call (469) 270-8156.
  6. Fill in an Incident Report the same day.
  7. Maa's Place is not a care program: no medicines are given by staff or volunteers.

3. Severe weather or tornado

  1. The person in charge watches weather alerts on days with storms in the forecast.
  2. Tornado warning or sirens: move everyone to the shelter area (interior room, away from windows).
  3. Sit low, cover heads and necks. Help people with walkers or wheelchairs first.
  4. Do a head count with the attendance list.
  5. Stay there until the warning ends. Do not leave in a storm.
  6. After it passes: check for injuries, call 911 if needed, then call (469) 270-8156.

4. Lockdown (danger inside or outside)

  1. If there is a threat, call 911 when it is safe.
  2. Lock or block doors. Turn off lights. Stay away from windows and doors. Silence phones.
  3. Keep everyone quiet and together. Do not open the door for anyone until police say it is safe.
  4. If you can get out safely and quickly, leave and go far from the danger.
  5. When police arrive, keep hands visible and follow their directions.
  6. Afterwards: head count, call (469) 270-8156, Incident Report.

5. Lost or missing participant

  1. As soon as someone is missing, tell the person in charge. Check the whole suite: restrooms, rooms, hallways, parking area.
  2. If not found within 10 minutes, or right away if the person has memory loss or is at risk: call 911.
  3. Give police a description: name, age, clothes, when last seen.
  4. Call the participant's emergency contact, then (469) 270-8156.
  5. One person stays with the rest of the group; keep doing head counts.
  6. Fill in an Incident Report the same day.

6. Power outage

  1. Stay calm. Get the flashlights. Keep people seated until there is light.
  2. Check for anyone stuck or hurt. If there is fire, smoke or sparks, follow Fire steps and call 911.
  3. Report the outage to building management and call (469) 270-8156.
  4. If the power is not back soon, or the room gets too hot or too cold, the person in charge may close early and call families or emergency contacts.
  5. Do not use candles.

After any emergency

  • Do a final head count.
  • Fill in an Incident Report before you leave that day.
  • Tell Aslam Hemani at (469) 270-8156.
  • Bloom Village: follow its own reporting rules (Bloom Village Emergency Preparedness Plan and Incident Report Guide).

Practice

  • Practice a fire evacuation and a tornado drill at least once a year (Bloom Village follows its own drill schedule).
  • Walk through the space with every new staff member and volunteer: exits, meeting spot, shelter area, first-aid kit, AED, extinguishers.
