Text Message Terms

What texts you get, how you agree, and how to stop. Version text-terms-v3.3-2026-10-19.

Questions? Call (469) 270-8156.

FromMaa’s Inspiration Inc. (Maa’s Meals)
WhatInformational texts about volunteering, deliveries and programs, such as the Thursday “are you available this Saturday?” question, a Friday reminder if there is no reply, a confirmation of your reply, your Saturday assignment (deliver or packing), and a thank-you after the shift.
How oftenMessage frequency varies.
CostMessage and data rates may apply.
To stopReply STOP (or STOPALL, UNSUBSCRIBE, CANCEL, END, QUIT) at any time. You will get one confirmation and no further texts. Reply START to opt back in.
For helpReply HELP, call (469) 270-8156, or email aslam@maasinspiration.org.
How you agreeBy giving your phone number and agreeing to the terms of one of our forms (volunteer sign-up, driver agreement, Maa’s Place sign-up, meal recipient form or Bloom Village fee agreement), you agree to receive informational texts from Maa’s Inspiration Inc. about volunteering, deliveries and programs. You can opt out at any time.
Not requiredConsent to texts is not a condition of receiving services or volunteering. Reply STOP or call us and you can still take part.
PrivacyWe do not sell or share mobile numbers or text opt-in information with anyone for marketing. See our Privacy Policy and Terms of Use.

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. If your phone number changes, please sign up again with the new number.
