Cash & Check Handling Procedure

How we receive, deposit and record every cash gift and check. Draft v2 · October 19, 2026.

Who does what

No one person handles a gift from start to finish. The person who records or reconciles is never the only person who deposited.

The 9 rules

  1. Write a receipt from the numbered receipt book for every cash gift or payment, and whenever asked. Keep the copy.
  2. Endorse checks right away: stamp the back “For deposit only – Maa's Inspiration Inc.” Never cash a check.
  3. Log it: every item goes on the Deposit Log (last page).
  4. Lock it up in the locked cash box, in a locked place, until it is deposited.
  5. Deposit within 2 business days. The depositor keeps the bank receipt and gives it to the recorder.
  6. No cash at home, in a car overnight, or in a personal account. No borrowing or making change from your own money.
  7. Do not pay bills from cash. Expenses are paid from the organization's account, approved, with a receipt.
  8. Record it in the Interim Ledger (Income and Deposits tabs) within 2 business days, tagged with the program.
  9. Amounts do not match? Never cover it with your own money. Write the difference on the Deposit Log and tell the approver the same day.

Card gifts (Stripe), reconciliation and thank-you letters

Stripe (online card gifts) – each month

  • Download the Stripe payouts report for the month.
  • Match each Stripe payout to a bank deposit. Stripe pays out the net amount; record the gross gift as income and the Stripe fee as an expense.
  • Check every gift in Stripe is in the Income tab (program tag, donor, date).
  • Note refunds and disputes. Ask the approver about anything you cannot match.

Monthly reconciliation (by the 10th of the next month)

  • The reconciler (not the depositor) compares the bank statement to the Deposits tab, the Stripe payouts and the Expenses tab.
  • Every deposit has a Deposit Log entry and a bank receipt. Every expense has a receipt and an approval.
  • The Monthly Summary tab shows income and expenses by program. Differences are explained in writing.
  • The reconciler and the approver sign and date the month (bottom of this page).

Donor thank-you and acknowledgment letters

  • Use the Donor Acknowledgment Letter Templates (Batch 2, doc 03). Every letter shows Maa's Inspiration Inc., 501(c)(3), EIN 86-2221897.
  • Gifts of $250 or more need a written acknowledgment (IRC §170(f)(8)), stating the amount and whether anything was given in return. We aim to thank every donor within 48 hours.
  • Payments over $75 where the donor gets something (a dinner, an event ticket): use Letter B with a good-faith value of what they received (IRC §6115).
  • A Stripe email receipt alone is not enough for $250+ gifts. Send the letter too.
  • Record the date the letter was sent in the Income tab (column “Ack sent”). Year-end statements go out by January 31.
  • Non-cash gifts (food, supplies): describe the item, but do not put a dollar value on it. That is the donor's job.

Keep the records

Keep deposit slips, bank receipts, receipt-book copies, Stripe reports and letters for 7 years (Board Policy Pack, record retention). Keep donor details private.
