Privacy, accidents and costs

Privacy

Recipients’ names, addresses, phone numbers and delivery photos are private. I will use them only to deliver meals. I will not share, copy, post or keep them after my route, and I will not contact recipients for personal reasons.

If there is an accident, ticket or problem

  1. Stop safely. Call 911 if anyone is hurt. Follow the law for exchanging information.
  2. Call the Maa’s Meals coordinator as soon as it is safe.
  3. Report the accident to my own insurance company.
  4. Tell Maa’s in writing within 24 hours about any accident, injury or traffic ticket that happened while volunteering, and within 7 days if I get a ticket or my license changes at any other time.

Costs

Volunteer driving is unpaid. Maa’s Inspiration Inc. does not pay for gas, mileage, tolls, parking, tickets or vehicle damage unless approved in writing before the trip. Some mileage for charity driving may be tax-deductible – ask your tax adviser.
