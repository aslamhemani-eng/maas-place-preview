Maa’s Place Terms & Waiver

Participation waiver and release for Maa’s Place. Version maas-place-terms-v3.3-2026-10-19.

Questions? Call (469) 270-8156.

Maa’s Place is a free center for seniors operated by Maa’s Inspiration Inc., a 501(c)(3) nonprofit at 2700 Old Denton Rd, Suite 102, Carrollton, TX 75007. Open Monday to Thursday, 10 AM to 2 PM. Ages 55 and up.

1. Voluntary participation

I am choosing to take part in Maa’s Place activities, which may include light exercise, chair yoga, walks, games, arts and crafts, outings, and meals or snacks. Participation is voluntary and I may stop at any time.

2. Health and fitness

I am able to take part in these activities. I will consult my doctor if I have any doubts, and I will tell staff about any health, mobility, or dietary needs. Maa’s Place does not provide medical care, supervision for memory care, or transportation.

3. Risks

Physical activities and outings carry some risk of injury, including falls, strains, and illness. I accept these risks.

4. Release of liability

I RELEASE MAA’S INSPIRATION INC., ITS DIRECTORS, VOLUNTEERS, AND STAFF FROM ANY CLAIMS FOR INJURY, ILLNESS, OR LOSS ARISING FROM MY PARTICIPATION, INCLUDING CLAIMS ARISING FROM THEIR OWN NEGLIGENCE, TO THE FULLEST EXTENT ALLOWED BY TEXAS LAW.

5. Emergencies

In an emergency, I authorize staff to call 911 and contact my emergency contact. I am responsible for any resulting medical costs.

6. Food

I will tell staff about any food allergies. Food may be prepared in a kitchen that also handles common allergens.

7. Signing for someone else

A family member or guardian may sign on the participant’s behalf and agrees to these terms for them.

8. Texts, Photos & Video

  • Texts: by giving a phone number and agreeing to these terms, you agree to receive informational text messages from Maa’s Inspiration Inc. about volunteering, deliveries and programs. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out and HELP for help. Consent is not a condition of receiving services. See our Text Message Terms.
  • Photos and video: Maa’s may take photos and video at programs and events and use them on its website, social media and materials. If you do not want to be photographed or featured, tell us at (469) 270-8156 or aslam@maasinspiration.org and we will honor it.
