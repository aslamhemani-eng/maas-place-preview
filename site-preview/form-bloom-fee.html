Read the Bloom Village Fee Terms & Policies

1. Program days and hours

  • Bloom Village is open Monday to Friday, 9:00 AM to 1:00 PM. Pick up is at 1:00 PM.
  • During the pilot, participants attend 2 or 3 days a week, with up to 8 adults in the group.
  • To change days, ask the administrator. Changes start when space and staffing allow.

  • The $100 enrollment fee is non-refundable. It is not returned if the participant does not start, leaves the program, or is discharged. We will not collect it until this agreement is signed.
  • Pay by check to Maa’s Inspiration Inc., online payment, or another way agreed with us. We give a receipt for every payment.

3. Absences, holidays and closures

  • Absences are not charged. Please tell us as early as you can.
  • Closed on all major holidays (no charge).
  • Closures for weather or emergencies are not charged.

4. Late payment and pickup

  • There is no late payment fee and no late pickup fee.
  • If tuition is not paid by the due date, we send a written reminder. If it is still not paid 15 days after the reminder, we will talk with you about a plan. Services may end only after reasonable written notices and the discharge steps below.
  • Only people on the authorized pickup list may take the participant home. Bring photo ID until we know you.

5. Leaving the program and refunds

  • Please give us 15 days’ written notice before leaving.
  • Prepaid program days not yet provided are refunded, minus any balance owed. There is no deadline for this refund. The $100 enrollment fee is not refunded.
  • If Bloom Village must end services, we give at least 30 days’ written notice, except in an emergency. You may use the complaint procedure or call HHSC at 1-800-458-9858 about any discharge.

6. Tuition assistance (scholarships)

Need-based help may be available as funds allow: Bloom 25%, Hope 50%, Legacy 75%, Full Bloom up to 100%. It is not guaranteed, and the $100 enrollment fee is not automatically covered. Awards are reviewed once every year. Call us to ask for the application.

7. Medicare and Medicaid

Within 30 days after starting, we will tell you if Medicare or Medicaid may cover any of our services.

8. Your agreement

By agreeing, you confirm you have read these terms, had your questions answered, and agree to them for the participant named on the form. You will receive a copy.

9. Texts, Photos & Video

  • Texts: by giving a phone number and agreeing to these terms, you agree to receive informational text messages from Maa’s Inspiration Inc. about volunteering, deliveries and programs. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out and HELP for help. Consent is not a condition of receiving services. See our Text Message Terms.
  • Photos and video: Maa’s may take photos and video at Bloom Village. Photos of participants are used publicly (website, social media, materials) only with the guardian’s agreement under these terms. The guardian can opt out at any time by calling (469) 270-8156 or emailing aslam@maasinspiration.org, and we will honor it.
