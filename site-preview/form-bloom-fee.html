Absences, payment and leaving

Absences, holidays and closures

  • Absences are not charged. Please tell us as early as you can.
  • Closed on all major holidays (no charge).
  • Closures for weather or emergencies are not charged.
  • To change days, ask the administrator. Changes start when space and staffing allow.

Late payment

  • There is no late payment fee and no late pickup fee.
  • If tuition is not paid by the due date, we send a written reminder. If it is still not paid 15 days after the reminder, we will talk with you about a plan. Services may end only after reasonable written notices and the discharge steps.

Leaving the program and refunds

  • Please give us 15 days’ written notice before leaving.
  • Prepaid program days not yet provided are refunded, minus any balance owed. There is no deadline for this refund. The $100 enrollment fee is not refunded.
  • If Bloom Village must end services, we give at least 30 days’ written notice, except in an emergency. You may use the complaint (grievance) procedure or call HHSC at 1-800-458-9858 about any discharge.

Tuition assistance (scholarships)

Need-based help may be available as funds allow: Bloom 25%, Hope 50%, Legacy 75%, Full Bloom up to 100%. It is not guaranteed, and the $100 enrollment fee is not automatically covered. Awards are reviewed once every year.

Medicare and Medicaid

Within 30 days after starting, we will tell you if Medicare or Medicaid may cover any of our services.
