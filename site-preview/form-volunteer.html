Read the Volunteer Terms, Policies & Waiver

1. Code of conduct

  • Dignity first: treat every guest, senior, family and fellow volunteer with kindness and respect – Love All, Serve All.
  • Show up: arrive on time, check in and check out with the shift lead, and let us know early if you can’t make it.
  • Food safety: wash hands, wear gloves and hair covering in the kitchen, and follow the lead’s instructions.
  • Privacy: keep guests’ personal information private. Do not take personal photos of guests; take photos only when Maa’s asks, and never of anyone who has opted out.
  • Safety: no alcohol, drugs, smoking or weapons at any site. Drivers must also agree to the Driver Terms, Policies & Waiver.
  • No harassment or discrimination of any kind. Minors volunteer only with guardian approval and adult supervision.
  • Ask for help: report any concern or injury to the shift lead or Aslam right away.

2. Volunteer Waiver & Release

  • Assumption of risk. Volunteering can involve lifting and carrying boxes and supplies; kitchen work and food handling, including hot surfaces, sharp tools and common food allergens; standing, walking and deliveries; and outings and events. These activities carry some risk of injury or illness. I understand these risks, I choose to accept them, and I will follow instructions and stop if something feels unsafe.
  • Release and hold harmless. TO THE EXTENT TEXAS LAW ALLOWS, I RELEASE AND AGREE TO HOLD HARMLESS MAA’S INSPIRATION INC., ITS DIRECTORS, OFFICERS, STAFF AND VOLUNTEERS FROM ANY CLAIMS FOR INJURY, ILLNESS, LOSS OR DAMAGE ARISING FROM MY VOLUNTEERING, INCLUDING CLAIMS ARISING FROM THEIR OWN NEGLIGENCE.
  • No pay. I am a volunteer, not an employee. I will not receive wages or employee benefits for my time.
  • Emergency medical consent. In an emergency, I authorize Maa’s Inspiration staff and volunteers to call 911, to get emergency medical care for me, and to contact my emergency contact if I have given one. I am responsible for the cost of that care.
  • Own health insurance. Maa’s Inspiration does not provide health or accident insurance for volunteers. I will rely on my own health insurance and am responsible for my own medical costs.
  • Volunteers under 18. A parent or guardian must agree to these terms and sign the sign-up form for a volunteer under 18, and accepts this waiver and release for the minor to the extent the law allows. Volunteers under 18 must be supervised by an adult.

3. Text messages

  • By giving your phone number and agreeing to these terms, you agree to receive informational texts from Maa’s Inspiration Inc. about volunteering, shifts and programs (for example, “are you available this Saturday?” and shift reminders).
  • Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply.
  • To stop: reply STOP (or STOPALL, UNSUBSCRIBE, CANCEL, END, QUIT) at any time. You will get one confirmation and no further texts. Reply START to opt back in.
  • For help: reply HELP, call (469) 270-8156 or email aslam@maasinspiration.org.
  • Texts are not a condition of volunteering. We do not sell or share mobile numbers or text opt-in information with anyone for marketing. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. See our Text Message Terms.

4. Photos and video

  • Photos and video: Maa’s may take photos and video at programs and events and use them on its website, social media and materials. If you do not want to be photographed or featured, tell us at (469) 270-8156 or aslam@maasinspiration.org and we will honor it.
  • Volunteers must not post photos of seniors, guests or meal recipients on personal accounts.

5. Bloom Village

Bloom Village begins only after its HHSC license is issued. Bloom Village volunteers must be 18 or older and complete screening and training. We will contact you.

6. How to opt out

  • Texts: reply STOP to any text, or call us. Reply HELP for help.
  • Photos, video and anything else in these terms you can opt out of: call (469) 270-8156 or email aslam@maasinspiration.org. Tell us what you want to stop and we will honor it.
  • Opting out does not affect your services or your place in the program.
