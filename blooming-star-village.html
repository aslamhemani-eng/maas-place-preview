Our Mission

Every person deserves a place where they are understood, included and helped to grow. A free recreation program, Blooming Star Village carries Maa's spirit of unconditional care to neurodiverse individuals and the families who love them, offering a calm, supportive community so no family has to find its way alone.

🌱 Belonging

A place where differences are welcomed, not just accommodated.

🤝 Family support

Caring for the whole family, including parents, siblings and caregivers.

⭐ Growth

Helping each person build skills and confidence at their own pace.

Who We Serve

Blooming Star Village is planned for individuals with sensory processing differences, autism, developmental delays and other special needs, together with their families, across Dallas–Fort Worth.

Where is it located?

2700 Old Denton Rd, Suite 102, Carrollton, TX 75006 (same location as MAA's Inspiration)

What ages do you serve?

Ages served

Is there a cost?

No. Blooming Star Village is free.

Is this therapy?

No. Blooming Star Village is a recreation program: fun, inclusive activities and community. It does not provide therapy or medical services.

Do you provide transportation?

No, families arrange their own transportation.
