Privacy Policy

How Maa's Inspiration Inc. collects and uses your information.

This Privacy Policy applies to Maa's Inspiration Inc., a 501(c)(3) nonprofit (EIN 86-2221897) in Carrollton, Texas, including our Maa's Meals program, Senior Center and Bloom Village.

What data we collect

  • Name
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • Address (for meal delivery)

How we use it

  • Volunteer scheduling, including shift and route reminders
  • Meal delivery notices to meal recipients
  • Answering questions you send us

Text messages (SMS)

If you opt in to text messages from Maa's Inspiration Inc., we use your phone number only to send the messages described in our Terms & Conditions. You can reply STOP at any time to opt out, or HELP for help.

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Keeping your data safe

Only authorized Maa's Inspiration staff and volunteers can see your information, and only what they need for their role.
