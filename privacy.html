Privacy Policy

How Maa's Inspiration Inc. collects, uses and protects your information. Last updated October 19, 2026.

This policy covers Maa's Inspiration Inc., a 501(c)(3) nonprofit (EIN 86-2221897) in Carrollton, Texas, and its programs: Maa's Meals, Maa's Place and Bloom Village.

1. What we collect

  • Forms you send us: your name, phone, email, address and anything you type in a form, such as delivery notes or a complaint.
  • Volunteers: contact details, which programs you help with, and your agreement to our volunteer and driver terms.
  • Meal recipients: name, address, phone and delivery notes.
  • Bloom Village families: the participant's and guardian's names, phone and program days. Health and other private details are collected in person, not on this website.
  • Donations: online gifts are processed by Stripe. Stripe handles your card details; we never see or store your full card number. We receive your name, email, amount and date.
  • Delivery door photos: volunteer drivers take one photo at each stop showing only the meal at the door and the house number. These photos are deleted after 7 days.

2. How we use it

  • To run our programs: deliver meals, schedule volunteers, enroll participants and answer your questions.
  • To send receipts and thank-you notes for donations.
  • To keep records the law requires, such as signed agreements and licensing records.

3. We do not sell your information

We never sell, rent or trade your personal information.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties.

We share information only with service providers who help us run our programs (for example our secure database, text message and payment providers), and only as needed, or when the law requires it.

4. Text messages (SMS)

  • How you agree: when you give us your phone number on one of our forms and agree to that form's terms, you agree to receive informational texts from Maa's Inspiration Inc. about volunteering, deliveries and programs.
  • Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply.
  • Reply STOP at any time to opt out. Reply HELP for help, or call (469) 270-8156.
  • Consent to texts is not a condition of receiving services or volunteering. See our Text Message Terms.

5. Donor confidentiality

Donor names, amounts and contact details are kept confidential. We do not publish a donor's name without permission, and we never sell or share donor lists.

6. Photos and videos

  • Our program terms explain that Maa's may take photos and video at programs and events and use them on its website, social media and materials. We use first names only.
  • If you do not want to be photographed or featured, call (469) 270-8156 or email aslam@maasinspiration.org at any time, and we will honor it.
  • Bloom Village participants appear publicly only with their guardian's agreement under the Bloom Village terms. The guardian can opt out at any time.
  • Delivery door photos are for delivery confirmation only. They are never published and are deleted after 7 days.

7. Keeping your information safe

Only authorized staff and volunteers can see your information, and only what they need for their role. We use secure, password-protected systems. No system is perfectly secure, so please call us if you think something is wrong.

8. Children

This website is not for children. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. Bloom Village serves adults 18 and older.

9. Your choices

You may ask to see, correct or delete your information, or stop texts or emails, by contacting us. Some records must be kept by law.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
