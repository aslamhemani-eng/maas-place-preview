Maa's Place Participation Waiver and Release

Maa's Place is a free senior center operated by MAA's Inspiration Inc., a 501(c)(3) nonprofit located at 2700 Old Denton Rd, Suite 102, Carrollton, TX 75006.

1. Voluntary participation.

I am choosing to take part in Maa's Place activities, which may include light exercise, chair yoga, walks, games, arts and crafts, outings, and meals or snacks. I understand that participation is voluntary and that I may stop at any time.

2. Health and fitness.

I confirm that I am able to take part in these activities. I will consult my doctor if I have any doubts, and I will tell staff about any health, mobility, or dietary needs. I understand that Maa's Place does not provide medical care, supervision for memory care, or transportation.

3. Risks.

I understand that physical activities and outings carry some risk of injury, including falls, strains, and illness. I accept these risks.

4. RELEASE OF LIABILITY.

I RELEASE MAA'S INSPIRATION INC., ITS DIRECTORS, VOLUNTEERS, AND STAFF FROM ANY CLAIMS FOR INJURY, ILLNESS, OR LOSS ARISING FROM MY PARTICIPATION, INCLUDING CLAIMS ARISING FROM THEIR OWN NEGLIGENCE, TO THE FULLEST EXTENT ALLOWED BY TEXAS LAW.

5. Emergencies.

In an emergency, I authorize staff to call 911 and contact my emergency contact. I am responsible for any resulting medical costs.

6. Food.

I will tell staff about any food allergies. Food may be prepared in a kitchen that also handles common allergens.

7. Photo and video consent (optional).

☐ Yes, MAA's Inspiration may use photos and videos of me on its website and social media.
☐ No, please do not include me in photos or videos.

I may withdraw consent at any time by emailing aslam@maasinspiration.org.

8. Signature.

I have read and understand this waiver.
